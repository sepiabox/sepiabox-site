How it works

  1. Gather

    Start with your external drive: Sepiabox reads it without changing a file, and copies kept in several folders become one. Google Photos, Google Drive and OneDrive come later.

  2. Understand

    On arrival, each photo gets its date and place and, if you turn face recognition on, the people you name.

  3. Ask

    Questions become filters over that data, so the answer is instant and the photos are never read again.
