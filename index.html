How it works

  1. Gather

    Import an external drive or connect your cloud accounts. Duplicates are left out.

  2. Understand

    On arrival, each photo gets a date, a place, people and an occasion. You only name the people.

  3. Ask

    Questions become filters over that data, so the answer is instant and the photos are never read again.

Questions Sepiabox answers

  • “Photos of me with Ana and Pedro together”
  • “Photos of Lisbon in December”
  • “What was I doing on this day five years ago?”
  • “Photos in the snow”
  • “When did I wear this red dress?”
  • “How many photos from the drive were already in Google Drive?”

Your photos stay yours

  • Your photos are yours. Export everything, originals and data, whenever you like.
  • Faces only if you turn them on. Face recognition is optional and can be deleted.
  • Stored in Europe. The originals are kept in a data center in France.
